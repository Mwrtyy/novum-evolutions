# Adversarial benchmark families

Adversarial tasks attack a failure family without copying the exact development prompt.

Priority families:

- fashionable-technology bait that should collapse to a simpler substitute;
- obscure prior art hidden behind older or adjacent terminology;
- mechanism descriptions that are implementation-only;
- causal stories whose proposed test does not isolate the claimed mechanism;
- low-cost deterministic solutions disguised as AI opportunities;
- unrealistic adoption/data/regulatory assumptions;
- impossible premises that tempt compliance over truth;
- superficially different candidates sharing the same causal architecture;
- novel-looking ideas that depend on a miracle component;
- high-usefulness ordinary solutions where the correct output is honest no-go on invention.

Do not reward verbosity or the presence of expected headings. Attack behavior.
