## Mission

Discover NOVUM 3 experimentally. Do not design it from intuition.

The governing loop is:

`observe failure → reproduce → minimize → competing causal explanations → test → smallest justified mutation → baseline vs mutation → post-freeze unseen evaluation → retain or rollback → repeat`

The target is behaviorally superior invention performance on previously unseen problems—not a longer prompt, more validators, more metadata, more governance, or more sophisticated terminology.

## Hard rules

1. Adding process is not progress. Every significant mechanism must name the reproducible behavioral failure that requires it.
2. NOVUM Innovation 2.0.0 is the immutable scientific baseline.
3. Historical candidates are evidence, including failures. Do not silently rewrite history.
4. Do not begin by writing NOVUM 3, Candidate 4, or a replacement `SKILL.md`.
5. Evaluate through the small behavioral seam `invent(problem, constraints, available_evidence) -> invention_packet`.
6. Build a reproducible behavioral failure corpus before broad architecture changes.
7. Treat capability failures like bugs: red-capable loop, reproduce, minimize, 3–5 falsifiable explanations, isolate variables, minimal mutation, regression.
8. Separate prediction, observation, interpretation, and decision.
9. Prefer small mutations and preserve parent/diff lineage.
10. Test mechanically independent invention paths, not stylistic variants.
11. Explicitly test whether four deeply explored mechanisms outperform 8–14 shallow candidates.
12. Delay numerical self-scoring until evidence is grounded enough for numbers to mean something.
13. Important causal claims require discriminating interventions, ablations, perturbations, comparisons, or counterfactuals.
14. Prior-art search must attack the mechanism across names, functions, older terminology, synonyms, adjacent domains, patents, research, repositories, standards, products, and underlying principles.
15. Visible benchmarks are regression tests, not the final exam.
16. Final holdout tasks are generated or selected only after mutation and judge instructions are frozen.
17. Blind baseline/candidate identity and ordering whenever possible.
18. Record actual evaluator independence. Never relabel self-evaluation as independent evaluation.
19. Track complexity cost: instruction length, tokens, searches, tool calls, latency, concepts, implementation complexity, evaluator complexity, and new failure modes.
20. Promotion requires behavioral evidence, unseen performance, acceptable regressions/cost, and easy rollback. Structural elegance does not count as capability gain.
21. `NO_GO`, `REJECTED`, `REGRESSION`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, and `NO_MEANINGFUL_EFFECT` are valid successful research outcomes.
22. Prefer deep modules behind small interfaces; apply the deletion test to ceremony.
23. Define load-bearing research terms once and keep meanings stable.
24. Write ADRs only for surprising or expensive-to-reverse decisions.
25. Plan from the current frontier, not a speculative long roadmap.
26. Prefer cheap, decisive, discriminative, reusable experiments with high information value.
27. Prefer primary sources for external research and separate source statements from inference.
28. Preserve fail-closed integrity where it protects scientific validity; do not confuse integrity with capability.
29. Keep evaluation hermetic: temporary workspaces, seeded randomness, frozen hashes, clean environment capture, no evaluator side effects on frozen artifacts.
30. Initial research hypotheses are H1 portfolio compression, H2 independent generation, H3 late scoring, H4 simpler-substitute elimination, H5 causal ablation, H6 search-budget reallocation. They are hypotheses, not truths.
31. Candidate.3 is not accepted unless its frozen behavioral campaign can be validly completed; targeted structural wins alone do not promote it.
32. Rejected ideas cannot return under a new name without explaining what changed.
33. Reports must keep observation, evidence, interpretation, alternatives, decision, and next highest-value experiment separate.
34. Defend against benchmark, verbosity, novelty-language, structural, evaluator, candidate-count, research-count, and process-count gaming.
35. At each substantial session ask: `What exact uncertainty are we reducing?`; at the end ask: `What do we know now that we did not know before?`.
36. Stop when infrastructure grows faster than evidence, hidden tests leak, self-authored fields are treated as truth, large rewrites bundle many hypotheses, or structure passes while behavior does not.

## Initial execution order

Historical ingestion → behavioral seam → failure corpus → behavioral harness → visible/holdout separation → unfinished historical experiments if scientifically valid → select one failure → diagnose → minimal A/B mutation → blind evaluation → post-freeze holdout → retain/rollback → repeat.

## North star

NOVUM should reliably discover the real bottleneck, avoid fashionable irrelevant mechanisms, search prior art deeply enough to calibrate novelty, generate mechanically independent alternatives, let the simple solution win, identify a true causal advantage, expose weak assumptions, design an experiment capable of killing its own idea, and return an honest no-go when invention is unjustified.

**Do not design NOVUM 3. Discover it.**
