# EXP-001 final report — portfolio compression

Status: `EXP_001_COMPLETE`

Verdict: `REJECT`

## Decision

Do not promote the fixed four-candidate mutation and do not mutate the accepted production skill.

The corrected visible rerun was neutral, and the strict hidden holdout favored the frozen NOVUM 2.0.0 baseline. The candidate did not improve the two intended targets—mechanism novelty and mechanistic depth—at matched output length. Its small gains in simpler-substitute discipline, constraint fit, and falsifiability were within uncertainty and did not translate into overall preference.

`REJECT` applies to this frozen artifact and its global fixed-four policy. It does not establish that four candidates are always worse.

## Evidence summary

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Corrected visible rerun | 11 | 11 | 2 | 34 | 34 | 4 |

The historical visible aggregation (15–6–3; 43–22–7 votes) was generated by three persistent judging contexts. It remains an audit artifact but is superseded by the 72-fresh-context rerun for confirmatory interpretation.

The visible rerun's candidate win rate was 0.500. The hidden rate was 0.397 with a pair-cluster bootstrap 95% interval of [0.238, 0.571]. Visible and hidden are deliberately not pooled.

## Intended causal test

H1a predicted that compressing Standard-mode breadth from 8–14 initial candidates to exactly four, while reallocating the budget to collision checking and deepening, would improve novelty and mechanistic depth without harming usefulness.

Hidden mean candidate-minus-baseline deltas were:

- mechanism novelty: -0.067;
- mechanistic depth: -0.021;
- usefulness: -0.003;
- constraint fit: +0.019;
- falsifiability: +0.014;
- simpler-substitute discipline: +0.086.

Every 95% interval crossed zero. The primary targets were directionally negative, while the preference outcome was 14–7 for baseline. H1a is not supported.

## What the heterogeneity says

The candidate won two domains—physical system and product strategy—and lost five. It lost every pair in the economic-system and public-service cases. The plausible next hypothesis is not “four is best” or “breadth is always best”; it is that the useful breadth/depth trade changes with task structure.

This motivates an EXP-002 draft for a frozen adaptive expansion gate: begin with four candidates, but expand the portfolio when mechanism diversity or collision evidence remains insufficient. That proposal is not a production mutation and has no evidentiary status yet.

## Resource parity

Baseline outputs averaged 1,815 words; candidate outputs averaged 1,809. Baseline self-reported 7.62 tool calls per output; candidate 8.43. The candidate therefore did not win by being materially shorter or cheaper.

## Protocol judgment

The hidden holdout passed the lock/reveal sequence with 42 fresh generation contexts and 63 fresh evaluator contexts. Candidate, baseline, task set, generation configuration, and judge packet hashes were verified. The A/B mapping did not exist in evaluator-accessible storage until after the 63-judgment lock.

Limitations remain: all contexts used the same model family; tool/runtime metadata are self-reported; OS-level exclusion of the globally installed skill was unavailable; holdout structure and semantic selection were finalized after visible analysis, though the selector was isolated from the visible results. Two malformed generation pairs were discarded symmetrically and rerun before blinding. Materialized prompts added a single trailing line feed to the frozen JSON strings. None of these detected issues invalidates the within-system hidden comparison, but they constrain the claim.

## Final state

- accepted skill: unchanged NOVUM Innovation 2.0.0;
- candidate: rejected, retained as experimental artifact;
- production mutation: none;
- EXP-001 state: complete;
- next experiment: draft only, not authorized for execution or promotion.
